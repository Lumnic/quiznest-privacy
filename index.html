QuizNest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2025

QuizNest (“we”, “our app”, “the Service”) lets people answer trivia questions, compete on leader-boards and track their progress. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information we collect

CategoryDataPurpose
Account info Google e-mail, display name, profile photo (only when you sign in with Google) Create your QuizNest account & show your name/photo on leader-boards
Gameplay data Questions served, answers chosen, scores, categories played Show results & statistics, populate global leader-boards, improve question pool
Automatically collected Device model, OS version, language; app version, session length, screens viewed; Advertising ID Crash & usage analytics (Firebase); showing & measuring ads (AdMob)
Crash reports Stack traces, timestamp, device state at crash time Diagnose & fix bugs

We do not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or local files.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide core functions: gameplay, leader-boards, endless mode.
  • Personalise or limit ads via Google AdMob.
  • Analyse crashes and usage with Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics.
  • Send important service e-mails (e.g. policy changes) to signed-in users.

3. Sharing & third-party service providers

ProviderRole
Google Identity ServicesSingle-sign-on (Google Sign-in)
Google AdMobInterstitial & banner ads
Firebase (Google LLC)Auth backend, Cloud Functions, Crashlytics, Analytics

We never sell your personal data. Providers process it only to perform services on our behalf.

4. Data retention

  • Account & gameplay history — kept until you request deletion (see Section 6) or are inactive for 24 months.
  • Crash & analytics logs — 14 months (Firebase default).
  • Advertising ID — reset if you reset the ID in Android settings.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest on Google Cloud. Access is restricted to authorised staff under written confidentiality agreements.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account & data: email support@quiznest.app from your Google-signed-in address with the subject “Delete QuizNest account”. We erase your data within 7 days and confirm by e-mail.
  • Opt-out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
  • GDPR/CCPA rights (access, correction, portability): contact us at the same address.

Note: QuizNest currently requires Google Sign-in; playing without an account is not supported.

7. Children

QuizNest is not directed to children under 13. If we learn we collected personal data from a child, we delete it promptly.

8. Contact

QuizNest Support
E-mail: support@quiznest.app

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We’ll notify you in-app or by e-mail (for signed-in users) and post the new date at the top.
